# Semantic Copy & Information Architecture Rules

## Purpose

Prevent public copy that is grammatically correct but semantically inconsistent.

## Rules

1. **Parallel categories.** Items displayed in one group must be at comparable semantic levels. Split architecture disciplines, integration patterns, modernization work and quality attributes when needed.
2. **Technical taxonomy.** Do not treat providers, products, platforms, frameworks, protocols, patterns, engineering practices, architecture disciplines and quality attributes as interchangeable.
3. **Heading scope.** Every heading must accurately cover everything rendered beneath it. Change the heading, split the section or filter the data when it does not.
4. **Process sequencing.** A diagram creates causal and temporal meaning. Cross-cutting concerns such as security, permissions, traceability, observability, evaluation criteria and cost measurement must not be presented as a late stage merely to give them a box.
5. **Causality.** Do not state that one activity becomes another unless the transformation is literal. Training may lead to a subsequent discovery or pilot; it does not become one.
6. **Evidence classes.** Do not group teaching, founder roles, client projects, employer experience and public technical projects under a heading that implies they are all engineering case studies.
7. **UX semantics.** A control styled like a filter must filter, or be clearly presented as static taxonomy.
8. **Cross-route canonicalization.** The delivery process and capability taxonomy must not contradict across Home, Empresas, Jack, Casos and Academy. Reuse shared data where practical.
9. **Commercial clarity.** Precision must improve buyer understanding, not turn commercial pages into documentation.
10. **Human review.** A PR introducing a new technical taxonomy or process diagram must include a semantic-consistency review.

## PR checklist

- [ ] Headings cover their children.
- [ ] List items are semantically parallel.
- [ ] The process is technically defensible and controls are cross-cutting when appropriate.
- [ ] Technology taxonomy does not flatten unlike categories.
- [ ] Evidence classes are not mixed.
- [ ] Static visual controls do not imply interaction.
- [ ] Copy remains commercially readable on mobile and desktop.
