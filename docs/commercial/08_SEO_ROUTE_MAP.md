# SEO Route Map

## SEO principle

The site must be understandable by:
- executives using simple buyer language;
- technical buyers;
- search engines;
- AI/search assistants.

Do not keyword-stuff.

## Individual resource routes

For original content:
`/recursos/:slug`
or
`/recursos/videos/:slug`

Do not create thin pages for every keyword.

A provider-specific page is justified only when there is substantial original content and real commercial/search demand.

## Internal linking anchor language

Prefer natural descriptive anchors:
- soluciones de automatización con IA
- experiencia y proyectos
- perfil de Jack Aguilar
- formación corporativa en IA
- recursos sobre Agentic AI
- AI & Agentic SDLC

Avoid repeated "haz clic aquí".
