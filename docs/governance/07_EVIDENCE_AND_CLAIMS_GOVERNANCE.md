# Evidence & Claims Governance

## Evidence classes
- VERIFIED_RESULT
- PROFESSIONAL_EXPERIENCE
- CURRENT_ENGAGEMENT
- EXPERIMENT_OR_LAB
- RESEARCH
- HYPOTHESIS
- PIPELINE

Only `VERIFIED_RESULT` may be phrased as a demonstrated outcome without qualification.

## Engagement modes
- direct-client
- employer-project
- consulting-engagement
- anonymized
- personal-lab
- neuracode-project

Never imply a direct Neuracode/Jack client relationship for work performed through SDC Consulting or an employer.

## Metric ledger
Before publishing a number, record:
- claim;
- baseline;
- period;
- source;
- calculation;
- owner;
- caveats;
- publication permission.

## Case template
Context -> Problem -> Constraints -> Approach -> Architecture/Implementation -> Controls -> Result -> Evidence -> Lessons -> Status/publication notes

## Confidentiality
Never publish private architecture, non-public vulnerabilities, personal/health data,
private screenshots/messages, secrets/credentials, internal financial data or client material without permission.

## Health
Apply stricter privacy, safety, professional-judgment and human-oversight review.
Do not imply diagnosis/treatment capability unless it is genuinely in scope, lawful and evidence-backed.

## Testimonials
Only real, approved testimonials. No synthetic testimonials or invented paraphrases.

## Credentials
Verify exact credential title, issuer and status before badge rendering.

## Claim confidence check
For each claim in code/content, Codex should answer:
1. source?
2. evidence class?
3. allowed public wording?
4. permission?
5. last verified date?

If it cannot, downgrade or remove the claim.
