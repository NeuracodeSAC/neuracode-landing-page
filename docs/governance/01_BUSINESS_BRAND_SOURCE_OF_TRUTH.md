# Business & Brand Source of Truth

## Core thesis
Neuracode does not sell a specific LLM or hype. It solves business and engineering problems using the simplest architecture sufficient for the problem.

Principles:
- problem before tool;
- process before automation;
- evidence before claim;
- engineering before hype;
- deterministic workflow when sufficient;
- LLM when useful;
- agent only when agentic behavior is justified;
- governance proportional to risk;
- human review where business, safety or regulatory judgment requires it.
